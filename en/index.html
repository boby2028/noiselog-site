Recording

Start, review, then export what that version supports.

Shared wording follows the current site. Anything listed for one platform stays on that platform. Install conditions in the download section are the fuller note.

1

Start recording

Choose a trigger level, and calibrate when you need to. An event is created when the condition is met.

  • On a phone: leave it open and listening. Switch away, and it stops. Leaving the app, opening another app, or locking the screen stops listening.
  • iPhone: the China App Store description includes audio or video. Video can carry time, level, and an optional address watermark, and the clip goes into the on-device log after the trigger. A daily start and end can cross midnight. Listening and that schedule still need the app in front, and the first start is manual after you grant the permissions.
  • Android 1.0.3 (10): live decibels, on-device events, a manual address, video pre-roll, and a camera watermark. This build does not offer automatic location. Reading accuracy still needs a check on a real device.
  • Windows: a desktop monitoring test. Check the microphone, camera, and trigger save first.
  • Mac / iPad: no public download yet.

2

Review and organize

Look up the event time, peak, and average. Logs, media, and reports stay on the device.

  • A watermark can include the NoiseLog mark, time, and decibels. An address appears only if you enter one. You can clear local records in settings; export anything you want to keep first.
  • iPhone: you can look again at the stretch before and after the trigger. The store description also includes playback, duration, and making a spectrum image or a spectrum video with sound from saved audio, on the device. Pausing the graphics only pauses the display; listening continues.
  • The Android invite build keeps events on the device. The address is typed in.
  • On the Windows test, check that the log is still there after you close and reopen it.

3

Export

You choose when a file leaves the device. What can be exported depends on the platform and version.

  • iPhone: the store description includes exporting or sharing an on-device report when you ask. Supported share copies can hide the address; the original record stays as it was. Whether an analysis report and TXT export are in the current offer is shown in the App Store and in the app.
  • Android: export the records you want from the log. Save important videos one by one and confirm they play. Updates and a later move to a release build are on the help page.
  • Windows: check file export first. The help page also asks testers to check CSV. There is no PDF analysis and no subscription purchase.
  • Mac / iPad: no direct install link.

Limits

On the device, and clear about what a phone cannot claim.

The note stays here

  • No account is required.
  • Monitoring, media, events, and reports stay on the device.
  • Those records are not sent to a server run by the developer. The app has no ad or analytics SDK.
  • If you share, export, use a system backup, or pay through a store, that service handles the file under its own rules.
  • The current Android invite build does not request location. A watermark address is used only when you type one.
  • On iPhone you can leave the address empty or use system location. The store description says coordinates are not written into the local record. System reverse geocoding may use the network.
  • This website is static. It has no upload form, and it does not take payment or issue codes. The binding text is the privacy policy in Chinese.

A phone reading is a reference

  • Readings depend on the microphone, the input path, placement, and calibration. They are a reference.
  • They do not replace a metrologically certified sound level meter. This site does not promise certified accuracy.
  • Records and reports do not guarantee legal effect, and they do not guarantee that any organization will accept them.
  • Record only where the law allows, and give people any notice that place requires.
  • For Android 1.0.3 (10), compare a stable source with a sound level meter using the calibration prompt, and check readings with video on and off. An uncalibrated reading is an estimate.

FAQ

Short answers

Do I have to keep the app open?

Leave it open and listening. Switch away, and it stops. （开着听就行，切走会停。） Leaving the app, opening another app, or locking the screen stops listening. The China App Store description also mentions a daily start and end on iPhone. That schedule still needs the app in front, and the first start is manual.

Can I treat the decibel number as evidence?

Use it as a reference. The microphone, placement, and calibration all affect it, and it does not replace a certified sound level meter. This site does not promise certified accuracy, legal effect, or that any organization will accept the record. Record only where the law allows, and give any notice that place requires.

Are recordings uploaded?

Monitoring, media, and events stay on the device. The developer does not run a server that receives those records, and the app has no ad or analytics SDK. Sharing, export, system backup, and store payment are handled by the service you choose. If iPhone system location is used, reverse geocoding may use the network. That is not an upload of the recording to the developer.

Does every platform export the same file?

No single export applies everywhere. The iPhone store description includes exporting or sharing an on-device report, and supported share copies can hide the address. Analysis reports and TXT export follow the App Store and the in-app offer. The Android invite build exports kept records from the log. The Windows test asks you to check file export and CSV, and it has no PDF analysis. Mac and iPad are not available for download.

Is the Android test build the same package as the iPhone app?

No. Android is the 1.0.3 (10) invite test and still needs a time-limited authorization bound to the install code. iPhone is listed on the China mainland App Store. Install, update, and redemption steps are on the Chinese help page.
